From the Engineering of Promises to the Engineering of the State

The feasibility of the Authenticity and Modernity Party’s programme for the 2027–2031 legislature

Commentary

This note does not judge the programme on its intentions but on its capacity to be delivered. Its thesis fits in one phrase, the “paradox of the narrow mandate and the broad programme”: a programme designed for an absolute majority will have to be implemented by a coalition, on a limited electoral mandate and with a first budget prepared by the outgoing government.

2025TreatiseCriminal law

Treatise on Comparative Criminal Law. Toward a Post-National Criminal Law?

Crisis of territorial sovereignty and the reconfiguration of normative frameworks in the age of transnational crime

Commentary

This text runs counter to a teaching tradition that still presents criminal law as a finished national edifice. It starts from the major schools of penal thought to show that territoriality, the pillar of modern criminal law, is no longer enough when crime plays out across several continents and in digital space.
